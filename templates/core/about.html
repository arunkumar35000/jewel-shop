Our Heritage & Legacy

The Golden Legacy of Aura

Since 1982, Aura Luxury Jewellery has upheld the highest standards of Indian goldsmithing, preserving royal heritage while integrating cutting-edge 3D CAD design and interactive 360-degree digital previewing.

100% BIS Hallmarked Transparency

Every gram of gold at Aura is rigorously verified by Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) accredited assay centres. We laser-engrave the 6-character HUID (Hallmark Unique Identification) along with our official royal maker’s mark on every single piece.

Our pricing is 100% transparent:

  • Pure Gold Value: Net weight multiplied by today’s live bullion rate.
  • Making Charges: Reasonable craftsmanship charges explicitly listed on your tax invoice.
  • Stones & Diamonds: Billed strictly by weight and carat with official lab certificates.
  • GST (3%): Fully compliant Indian goods and service tax breakdown.
